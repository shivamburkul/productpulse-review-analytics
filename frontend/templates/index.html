ProductPulse — Understand Every Review.

Upload a CSV of customer reviews and ProductPulse shows what people feel, which features they talk about, how products compare, and how you can prove your dataset has not been changed.

  • Sentiment for every review
  • Feature-level insights for each product
  • Product comparison and trends
  • Dataset integrity fingerprint
CSV requirements
  • Header row with a product column (product, product_name) and a review text column (review, comment, text, review_text)
  • Optional: rating, date / review_date, review_id
  • .csv, up to 100 MB. Without dates, trends are shown as review groups.

    How It Works

    What is stored and what is checked.

    FingerprintEach row is turned into a SHA-256 hash. Hashes are combined in a Merkle tree into one root value for the whole file.
    LedgerOnly the root, file name, row count and time are stored, in an append-only chain of blocks on this computer. It is not Ethereum or any public network.
    VerificationThe current rows are hashed again. A different root means at least one row changed; the changed rows are listed.
    LimitsA mismatch shows that data differs from the registered version, not why. The ledger cannot tell whether a review is true.
